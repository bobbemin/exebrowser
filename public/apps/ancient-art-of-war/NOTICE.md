# The Ancient Art of War (1984)

Broderbund. Current rights holder unclear: created by Dave and Barry Murry (Evryware).

## Why this is hosted here

Abandoned software: The Ancient Art of War is not sold in any form today. It is hosted on the
same footing as the Internet Archive's software collections. That is a practical
position, not a legal claim; abandonment does not transfer copyright. The page
carries no advertising. If the rights holder asks, it is removed within 48 hours:
https://exebrowser.com/takedown/

Not sold: GOG, Steam and Antstream checked 2026-10-05; the 1984 original is not sold (the sequel, The Ancient Art of War in the Skies, is sold on Steam).
