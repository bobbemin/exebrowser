# Blockout (1989)

California Dreams. Current rights holder unclear: designers Aleksander Ustaszewski and Mirosław Zabłocki, publisher California Dreams defunct.

## Why this is hosted here

Abandoned software: Blockout is not sold in any form today. It is hosted on the
same footing as the Internet Archive's software collections. That is a practical
position, not a legal claim; abandonment does not transfer copyright. The page
carries no advertising. If the rights holder asks, it is removed within 48 hours:
https://exebrowser.com/takedown/

Not sold: GOG, Steam and Antstream checked 2026-10-05; the 1989 Blockout is not sold in any form.

## Source

Retrieved 2026-10-05 from https://archive.org/details/msdos_Blockout_1989
(Internet Archive item `msdos_Blockout_1989`; sha256 pinned in scripts/dos-sources.json).

The game files as preserved in that Internet Archive item, plus a
`.jsdos/dosbox.conf` DOSBox configuration, which is the only file added.
Archived copies of DOS games from this era have often had their copy
protection removed by a third party before they were preserved; this copy
is published as found, and nothing in it has been altered here.
