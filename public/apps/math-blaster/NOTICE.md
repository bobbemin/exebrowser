# New Math Blaster Plus (1990)

Davidson & Associates. Rights now held by JumpStart (NetDragon), which holds the Math Blaster name.

## Why this is hosted here

Abandoned software: New Math Blaster Plus is not sold in any form today. It is hosted on the
same footing as the Internet Archive's software collections. That is a practical
position, not a legal claim; abandonment does not transfer copyright. The page
carries no advertising. If the rights holder asks, it is removed within 48 hours:
https://exebrowser.com/takedown/

Not sold: GOG, Steam, Antstream and JumpStart's own site checked 2026-10-05; no Math Blaster game is on sale.
