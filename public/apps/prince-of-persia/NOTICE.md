# Prince of Persia (1990)

Broderbund. Rights now held by Ubisoft.

## Why this is hosted here

Abandoned software: Prince of Persia is not sold in any form today. It is hosted on the
same footing as the Internet Archive's software collections. That is a practical
position, not a legal claim; abandonment does not transfer copyright. The page
carries no advertising. If the rights holder asks, it is removed within 48 hours:
https://exebrowser.com/takedown/

Not sold: GOG, Steam, Ubisoft Store and Antstream checked 2026-10-05; the store listings named Prince of Persia are the 2008 game and later titles, and the 1990 original is not sold.
