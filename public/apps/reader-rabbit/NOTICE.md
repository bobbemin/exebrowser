# Reader Rabbit (1989)

The Learning Company. Rights now held by the successors to The Learning Company (HarperCollins).

## Why this is hosted here

Abandoned software: Reader Rabbit is not sold in any form today. It is hosted on the
same footing as the Internet Archive's software collections. That is a practical
position, not a legal claim; abandonment does not transfer copyright. The page
carries no advertising. If the rights holder asks, it is removed within 48 hours:
https://exebrowser.com/takedown/

Not sold: GOG, Steam, Antstream and HarperCollins/Houghton Mifflin Harcourt storefronts checked 2026-10-05; the 1989 Reader Rabbit is not on sale.

Retrieved 2026-10-05 from https://archive.org/details/msdos_Reader_Rabbit_1989
