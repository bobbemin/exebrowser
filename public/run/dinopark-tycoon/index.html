About DinoPark Tycoon

DinoPark Tycoon was made to teach money and management, and it went into elementary schools across the United States and Canada. Carnivores need meat and herbivores need plants, fences keep the dinosaurs in, and staff and food cost money every month. Visitor numbers rise and fall with the seasons, so a good summer has to carry you through a slow winter. Dinosaurs can escape, and the loan has to be repaid. The school edition starts with a bigger loan than the retail one.

Tips for getting started

Start small, and check what each purchase costs every month before you buy it.
