About Heimdall

Heimdall was one of Core Design's best-known games before Tomb Raider, first on the Amiga and then on DOS. The opening trials (an axe-throwing contest, a fight on a ship and a pig chase) decide your character's statistics. After that the game becomes an isometric role-playing adventure across three worlds, with a party of up to six Viking companions whose skills matter for different islands.
